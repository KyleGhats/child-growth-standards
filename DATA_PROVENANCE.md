# Data provenance

## DOH / NNC (age-in-months cutoffs)

CSV files under `sources/raw/doh/` support Philippine nutrition reporting (eOPT Plus–style band classification). They are **not** WHO LMS z-score tables.

## WHO Child Growth Standards

Xlsx files under `sources/raw/who/who-standard/` are derived from [WHO Child Growth Standards](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards) reference material (0–5 day/month tables and 5–19 year growth reference).

This package is **independent** and is **not** WHO-certified or endorsed software.
